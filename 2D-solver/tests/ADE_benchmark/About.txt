Tested with ADE_TST.py. Configurations:

4.0	# Lx = Ly [m]		
0.005	# grid spacing h [m]
0.05	# maximum simulation time t_max [s]
1100.0	# cutoff frequency f_cutoff [Hz]
22.0	# temperature Temp [°C]
0.5	# Courant-Friedrich-Lewy number C_CFL
12	# number of poles for the ADE (1 re 3 im)
(2,2)	# src_x, src_y
(3,2)	# rec_x, rec_y

In all simulations, the domain is excited with a plane wave with amplitude p0 = 0.0001 and u0 = 0.001.
IRnoise.bin is obtained with PMLs on all sides.
IR0.bin is obtained with a rigid boundary (R = 0.999 at all frequencies) on the east wall, periodic BCs above and below and PML on west wall.
IR1.bin is obtained with an adapted material fitted with vectFIT.py for the ADE on the east wall, periodic BCs above and below and PML on west wall.
Then the noise is subtracted from both signals and then their FFTs are divided so we can obtain the complex R
and compare to the analytical rational one fitted by the model.
