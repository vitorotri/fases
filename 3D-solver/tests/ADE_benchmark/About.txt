5.0				# Lx = Lz [m]
10.0			# Ly [m]	
(2.5, 5.0, 2.5)	# src_x, src_y
(2.5, 7.5, 2.5)	# rec_x, rec_y

Remaining simulation configurations are shown in config.txt.
In all simulations, the domain is excited with a PCS with amplitude p0 = 2.5.
IRnoise.bin is obtained with PMLs on all sides.
IR0.bin is obtained with a rigid boundary (R = 0.999 at all frequencies) on the north wall and PMLs on the remaining surfaces.
IR1.bin is obtained with an adapted material fitted with fminconFIT.py for the ADE on the north wall and PMLs on the remaining surfaces.
Then the noise is subtracted from both signals and then their FFTs are divided so we can obtain the complex R
and compare to the analytical rational one fitted by the model.
